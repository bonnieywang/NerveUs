What is NerveUs?

NerveUs is an online educational resource created to support the patient journey during peripheral nerve injury (PNI) treatment. This resource provides information on topics such as:

  • Nerve anatomy and symptoms of injury.
  • What to expect during clinical appointments.
  • The treatment and recovery process.
